# Subscription Detective Clubhouse

October 8, 2026. Subscription Detective is a Premium paid feature, included without charge during beta. No purchase SDK, client-controlled paid flag, bank detection, or automatic cancellation is activated.

- Home includes an illustrated Premium entry; the neighborhood's Detective Clubhouse treehouse is also tappable.
- Add/edit subscriptions and trials with recurring prices, frequency, renewal/trial dates and optional HTTPS provider settings links. Keep/Review/Cancelled decisions, active/cancelled filters, monthly/yearly cost projections and a projected avoided-cost casebook.
- The actual provider or app store must cancel the subscription. Marking it cancelled only changes the local case; linked bills remain separately managed.
- Cases persist in the existing encrypted finance vault and portable backups. Optional existing-bill references never create bills, change amounts, mark them paid, or affect Safe to Spend. Subscriptions without a bill must be added to Bills separately to reserve funds.
- Renewals advance one cycle only by user action, preserving month-end anchors and leap dates. Prices, dates and links are validated. Past dates remain visible for review.
- Opt-in private local reminders three days before and on saved dates, at 9 AM phone-local time. At most 12 upcoming Clubhouse notification days, separately namespaced from the existing 48 bill days. Cancellation/deletion/disabled settings reconcile only Clubhouse notifications. Backups restore reminders off.
- iOS/Android notification delivery and font scaling need device testing. Automatic detection and store-backed Premium access remain future work.
