# Premium beta — tester notes

Premium is included without charge during beta. Banking integrations are pending. Sample accounts, automation, alerts and credit scores are fictional; this build does not move money, pay bills or retrieve a credit report.

## Paste into TestFlight “What to Test”

Try the new diamond Premium tab and every illustrated feature button. Bobbie should appear in the upper right wearing her jeweled tiara and colorful gala gown. Compare the PayPlace logo with the locked original: white P, paw with teal checkmark, teal/yellow/purple/coral sections.

1. Open Account connections & beta setup, then connect the fictional sample accounts.
2. Round Up & Save: toggle sample round-ups on/off, choose a multiplier, save a weekly cap and simulate purchase batches. Savings must stop at the cap; turning the switch off must disable simulation. Invalid amounts must not save.
3. Life Happens Fund: save a goal and payday contribution, turn sample savings on/off, simulate a payday and a $50 emergency. The sample fund must stop at the goal and never go below zero.
4. Bill Shock Mode: enter an expense, compare Fastest/Balanced/Gentlest and save a recovery choice. Switch between your saved budget and the fictional budget. No bill should become Paid.
5. Help, I’m Falling Behind: check unpaid bills and priorities, try the sample budget and use Review my bills. Expected income must be labeled as an estimate.
6. Automatic Money Watch: refresh the fictional alerts, open Subscription Detective and return to the same Premium screen. Cancellation still happens with the subscription provider.
7. Credit Center: refresh the fictional score; confirm it is clearly labeled and separate from your manually saved score. The Home Credit Score button should open this screen directly.
8. Close/reopen PayPlace: preferences and sample activity should remain. Disconnect sample accounts: both automation switches should pause. Reset sample activity must keep real balances, bills and saved preferences.
9. Check every Back to Premium button and all six bottom tabs on a small phone, with the keyboard open and larger text enabled. Report clipping, missing images, taps that do nothing or a render crash.

Send feedback with the build number, feature, steps, expected result and actual result. Include a screenshot without personal financial information when helpful.
